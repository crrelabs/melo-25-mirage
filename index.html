Features & Amenities

Everything you need for the ultimate Miami lifestyle, right at home.

Rooftop Swimming Pool

Take in sweeping views of the Miami skyline from the rooftop pool deck. Lounge poolside with the downtown skyline as your backdrop — the perfect way to unwind after a long day in the city.

Fitness Center

Complement your healthy living regime with 25 Mirage's fully equipped fitness center featuring floor-to-ceiling windows with city views. From spin bikes to free weights and multi-station machines — everything you need for a complete workout without leaving home.

Designer Lobby

Arrive home to a beautifully appointed lobby featuring designer furnishings, modern artwork, and floor-to-ceiling glass that floods the space with natural light. It's the first impression of the luxury that awaits.

Panoramic Views

Every angle offers something stunning. From the downtown Miami skyline and Brickell's glittering towers to the colorful streets of Wynwood and beyond — 25 Mirage delivers views that make every day feel extraordinary.

Premium Finishes

Every unit comes equipped with granite countertops, rich wood cabinetry, full-sized appliances, and a private washer and dryer. Laminated wood floors in living areas and porcelain tiles in bathrooms complete the refined look.
